About Me

I am a PhD student at the Multi-Agent Robotics Vision and Learning (MARVL) Lab @ Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD). My emerging research interests focus on integrating physical principles into multi-agent collaboration to develop more capable, efficient, and robust autonomous systems.

I have experience in general robotics and autonomous systems. My research interests lie in optimal control, reinforcement learning, and physics-informed modeling.
